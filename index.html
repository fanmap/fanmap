Before you join

A FEW GOOD
QUESTIONS.

Do I have to be an alumnus?

No. Fan Map welcomes every fan. Joining Fan Map and joining an alumni association are separate choices.

Can I switch teams after signing up?

Your team is permanent for your Fan Map account. To choose another, you must delete your account and register again. We explain this before you confirm your team.

What is available to try now?

The review app includes team registration, directory browsing, RSVPs, tailgate pins, shared events, a fan board, news, and retailer links. This standalone preview uses a temporary demo profile. The hosted app requires sign-in; public Fan Map registration is not open yet.

Do shared invitations require an account?

Yes. Recipients sign in and complete Fan Map registration before seeing the gathering. They choose their own recipients and send through their messaging app.

Are maps available offline?

Downloadable offline map packs are planned. The current review includes a stadium sketch fallback and a straight-line compass, but does not promise offline reopening or navigation.

Is Fan Map affiliated with a university?

Fan Map is independent. Arkansas is the initial pilot focus; association participation and chapter recognition remain subject to the association’s approval.
